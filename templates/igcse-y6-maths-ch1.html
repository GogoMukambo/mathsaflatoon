1 Addition & Subtraction

Rules of Integers

  • Integers are positive and negative whole numbers, together with zero.
  • To subtract an integer, add its inverse.

2 Factors & Multiples

LCM and HCF

  • The highest common factor is the largest number that divides both numbers.
  • The lowest common multiple is the smallest shared multiple.

3 Divisibility & Roots

Tests and roots

  • A number is divisible by 3 when its digit sum is divisible by 3.
  • The square root of a square number is the number that was multiplied by itself.
