How To Play

Guess the 6-letter Wordle of the Day in 7 tries. Everyone gets the same word today; a new word appears tomorrow.

  • Each guess must be a valid 6-letter English word.
  • The color of the tiles will change to show how close your guess was to the word.

Examples

P L A N E T

P is in the word and in the correct spot.

S I L V E R

L is in the word but in the wrong spot.

B R I G H T

G is not in the word in any spot.
