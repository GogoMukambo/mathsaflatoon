Ratio and Proportion

Master simplifying ratios, sharing amounts, and using direct proportion.

1 Simplifying Ratios

A ratio compares quantities. You write a ratio in its simplest form by dividing the numbers by their highest common factor. A simplified ratio must not contain fractions or decimals!

Solved Example: Find the simplest form

Write the ratio 8:12 in its simplest form.

  • Find the highest common factor of 8 and 12, which is 4.
  • Divide both numbers by 4.
  • 8 ÷ 4 = 2
  • 12 ÷ 4 = 3

Answer: 2:3

2 Sharing in a Ratio

To share an amount in a given ratio, first add the numbers in the ratio to find the total number of parts. Then divide the amount to find the value of one part.

Solved Example: Share an amount

Share $9000 between Sally and Bob in the ratio 2:1.

  • Total number of parts: 2 + 1 = 3
  • Value of one part: $9000 ÷ 3 = $3000
  • Sally gets 2 parts: $3000 × 2 = $6000
  • Bob gets 1 part: $3000 × 1 = $3000
  • Check: $6000 + $3000 = $9000

Answer: Sally gets $6000, Bob gets $3000

3 Using Direct Proportion

Two quantities are in direct proportion when their ratio stays the same as they increase or decrease. You can use the unitary method (finding the cost of 1 item first).

Solved Example: Unitary Method

Three books cost $12. Work out the cost of 10 books.

  • Find the cost of one book: $12 ÷ 3 = $4
  • Find the cost of 10 books: 10 × $4 = $40

Answer: $40
