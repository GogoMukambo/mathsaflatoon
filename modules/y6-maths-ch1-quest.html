1 Addition & Subtraction

Rules of Integers

  • Integers are positive and negative whole numbers, together with zero.
  • Subtraction is the inverse operation of addition. To subtract an integer, you add the inverse.
  • You can estimate the answers to calculations by rounding the numbers to the nearest whole number or 10.

Solved Example

Work out: -5 + 9

Start at -5. Move 9 places to the right. You finish at 4. -5 + 9 = 4.

Work out: 2 - -5

Subtracting a negative adds the inverse. 2 + 5 = 7.

1 Multiplying and Dividing

Core Rules

  • Division is the inverse operation of multiplication.
  • The product of a positive and a negative integer is always negative.
  • When one integer is positive and the other is negative, the division answer is also negative.
  • Always calculate operations inside brackets first!

Solved Example

Work out: 4 × -8

4 groups of -8. 4 × -8 = -32.

Work out: -20 ÷ (3 - -2)

Bracket first: 3 + 2 = 5. Then -20 ÷ 5 = -4.

2 Lowest Common Multiples (LCM)

  • The multiples of a number are its times table (e.g., multiples of 4 are 4, 8, 12, 16...).
  • Common multiples are numbers found in both lists.
  • The lowest common multiple (LCM) of two numbers is the smallest number that is a multiple of both.

Solved Example: LCM of 6 and 9

  • Multiples of 6: 6, 12, 18, 24, 30, 36...
  • Multiples of 9: 9, 18, 27, 36...
  • The lowest common multiple is 18.

2 Highest Common Factors (HCF)

  • The factors of a number divide it exactly (e.g., factors of 18 are 1, 2, 3, 6, 9, 18).
  • The highest common factor (HCF) of two numbers is the largest factor of both numbers.
  • You can use a highest common factor to simplify a fraction as much as possible.

Solved Example: HCF of 24 and 80

  • Factors of 24: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24.
  • Find the highest factor that also divides 80.
  • 8 divides 80 (80 ÷ 8 = 10). 12 and 24 do not. HCF is 8.

3 Divisibility Rules

  • Divisible by 2 if the last digit is 0, 2, 4, 6 or 8. Divisible by 5 if it ends in 0 or 5.
  • Divisible by 3 if the sum of the digits is a multiple of 3. Divisible by 9 if the sum of the digits is divisible by 9.
  • Divisible by 4 if the number formed by the last two digits is divisible by 4.
  • Divisible by 6 when it is divisible by both 2 and 3.

Solved Example: Show 3948 is divisible by 3 and 6

  • Sum of digits: 3 + 9 + 4 + 8 = 24. 24 is a multiple of 3.
  • 3948 is even (ends in 8) and divisible by 3, so it is divisible by 6.
  • 24 is not divisible by 9, so 3948 is not divisible by 9.

3 Squares and Cubes

  • You use an index of 2 to show square numbers (e.g., $1^2 = 1, 2^2 = 4$). The symbol for square root is $\sqrt{}$.
  • You use an index of 3 to show cube numbers (e.g., $1^3 = 1, 2^3 = 8$). The symbol for cube root is $\sqrt[3]{}$.
  • You can easily estimate the square roots of integers that are not perfect square numbers.

Solved Example: Work out $\sqrt[3]{125} - \sqrt{49}$

  • $5^3 = 5 \times 5 \times 5 = 125$, so $\sqrt[3]{125} =$ 5.
  • $7^2 = 7 \times 7 = 49$, so $\sqrt{49} =$ 7.
  • Calculation: 5 - 7 = -2.
