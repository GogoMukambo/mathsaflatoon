1 Fractions, Decimals, & Percentages

You can write a percentage as a fraction or a decimal. For example, 32% = 32/100 = 0.32. You can then simplify the fraction: 32/100 = 8/25.

Solved Example: Calculating Area using %

In a circle diagram, 3 parts out of 10 are pink. The total area is 60 cm². What is the pink area?

  • 3 out of 10 is the fraction 3/10.
  • This is equivalent to 30/100, which is 30% or 0.3.
  • Using fraction: 3/10 × 60 = 60 ÷ 10 × 3 = 18 cm².
  • Using decimal: 0.3 × 60 = 18 cm².

Answer: 18 cm²

2 Percentages Large and Small

Percentages can be less than 1% (e.g., 0.5% = 0.005) or greater than 100%. A price increase of 75% means the new price is 175% of the old price.

Solved Example: Very Small % to Fraction

Write 17.5% as a fraction in its simplest form.

  • Write as a fraction over 100: 17.5/100
  • Multiply numerator and denominator by 10 to remove the decimal: 175/1000
  • Divide by common factor 5: 35/200
  • Divide by 5 again: 7/40

Answer: 7/40

Solved Example: Percentages > 100%

A child's height is 140% of its height 2 years ago (which was 70 cm). What is it now?

  • Using decimals: 140% = 1.4
  • Calculate: 1.4 × 70 = 98
  • Alternatively using fractions: 140% = 140/100 = 7/5
  • Calculate: 7/5 × 70 = 70 ÷ 5 × 7 = 14 × 7 = 98

Answer: 98 cm
