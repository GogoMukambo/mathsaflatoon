1 Respiration

How air gets into the lungs, how gases are exchanged, how breathing is controlled, and how cells release energy.

1.1 The human respiratory system

Nose and mouth, larynx (voice box, with the vocal cords), trachea (windpipe), bronchi, bronchioles and air sacs (alveoli), plus the diaphragm and rib muscles that move air.

Cartilage rings keep the trachea and bronchi open so air can always pass.

Dust and microbes stick to the mucus. Cilia sweep it up and out of the lungs.
