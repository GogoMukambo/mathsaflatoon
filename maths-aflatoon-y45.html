The Science of Mental Fluency

Why Year 5 Is the "Make-or-Break" Year for Maths Speed

In Year 5, children face multi-digit multiplication, improper fractions, percentages, and word problems. When a child takes 15 seconds just to work out 83 - 47, their working memory overloads and they lose track of the main problem.

1. Strategy Before Speed

Handing a child a timed worksheet without teaching them how to calculate mentally just makes them count on their fingers faster. Each of our 30 days starts with a visual "Brain Hack Lesson" that rewires how they see numbers.

2. The 50-Card Myelination Sprint

Neuroscience shows a new mental pathway becomes automatic after ~50 focused repetitions in a single session. Every day includes a custom 50-flashcard deck that progresses from gentle warm-ups (Cards 1–15) to boss-level mastery (Cards 35–50).

3. Gamified Beat-the-Clock Pride

Kids love beating their own record! With live streak counters, seconds-per-card telemetry, and 3-star milestones, children beg to play "just one more sprint" to turn a 2-star day into a 3-star Gold Trophy.
